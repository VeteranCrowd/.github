# VeteranCrowd

**Automatic cash back. Zero friction. Your service already earned it.**

[VeteranCrowd](https://www.veterancrowd.com/) is a veteran-founded community that turns military service into everyday rewards. Link the Visa card already in your wallet and earn cash back at participating merchants. No asking, no codes, no proving yourself at the register.

- Free for the military community: no fees, ever
- No new card: works with most Visa credit and debit cards
- Built on the Visa network
- Giving back to veteran service organizations

## How it works

1. **Join free.** Create your account and confirm your military service just once. It takes about 2 minutes.
2. **Link your Visa.** Link the Visa card you already carry. No new card, no new bank account.
3. **Shop normally.** Shop at participating merchants, in-store or online. Cash back is detected for you.

Cash back posts automatically and is available after a 30–45 day settlement window, which covers returns and chargebacks. Then it's yours to withdraw to your bank or give to a veteran service organization.

## Who's eligible

- **Active Duty, Guard and Reserve:** confirm your status with your .mil email address.
- **Veterans:** confirm your service once through the Department of Veterans Affairs.
- **Family members:** spouses and children join when an existing VeteranCrowd member sponsors them.

## Security and privacy

- **PCI DSS compliant:** the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard, the same standard banks and card networks follow.
- **Your card number stays with Visa:** your card is tokenized by Visa. VeteranCrowd never sees or stores your full card number.
- **Bank-grade encryption:** TLS 1.2 or better in transit and AES 256-bit encryption at rest.
- **Private by design:** merchants never see your military status, and we never sell your data.

## Who we are

Veterans working with veterans. VeteranCrowd was founded by Bob Louthan, a U.S. Army Reserve veteran, after he was turned away at a checkout counter in Richmond, Virginia, for asking about a military discount. He built the company so no veteran has to ask again.
